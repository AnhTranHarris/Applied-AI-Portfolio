# Applied AI Portfolio

I'm building practical experience with AI and learning where it can be useful in real work. This repository is where I keep projects, experiments, and notes connected to my Google AI studies.

## What I'm working on

- Practical uses of generative AI
- AI-assisted research and writing
- Simple workflow improvements
- Testing AI output instead of assuming it is correct
- Finding useful ways for people and AI to work together

## Current focus

I'm starting the Google AI Professional Certificate and building small projects alongside the coursework. I want the projects to show what I can actually do, not just what I have studied.

## Where I'm going

Over time, I want to build skills at the intersection of AI, technical communication, and better workplace processes.

See the [roadmap](ROADMAP.md) for the direction of the portfolio.

## A note about AI

AI may help me brainstorm, research, draft, edit, or test ideas. I review the final work myself and try to be clear about what I learned, what changed, and where the tools fell short.

## Projects

Projects will be added here as they are completed. I would rather have a few useful projects than a large collection of unfinished examples.
