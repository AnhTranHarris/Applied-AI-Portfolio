# Google AI Professional Portfolio

**Applied AI | Workplace AI | Generative AI | AI Workflow Design**

This repository is the applied-work portfolio for the **Google AI Professional Certificate** and the practical AI capabilities developed alongside it.

## Purpose

This is intentionally more than a course notebook. The goal is to demonstrate how AI knowledge can be translated into useful, repeatable workplace outcomes.

- AI workflow design
- Research and synthesis
- AI-assisted writing and communication
- Data and information analysis

## Portfolio Quality Standard

Every substantial artifact should answer:

1. What problem was being solved?
2. Why was AI appropriate?
3. What workflow or method was used?
4. How was the output verified?
5. What limitations or failure modes were identified?
6. What useful result was produced?
7. How could the approach be improved?

## AI Disclosure

AI-assisted work published here will be clearly identified. Final artifacts will be reviewed and validated by a human before publication. The objective is to demonstrate **responsible AI use**, not simply AI-generated output.
